# Scientific methodology
**Similarity.** Eight dimensions (geology, terrain, temperature, water/ice, dust, biology, caves, operations), each with its own metric, combined as S = sum(w_i S_i) over available dimensions with weights renormalised. Missing dimensions are excluded and reduce coverage; they are never zero. Output is an index, not a probability. Uncertainty interval: 5-95% over Monte-Carlo perturbation of measured features (measurement noise only). Sensitivity: Dirichlet weight perturbation.
**Three scores.** Analogue fidelity (environmental resemblance), mission suitability (access plus terrain safety; falls back to terrain-only and says so), transferability (heuristic discount by gravity, pressure, radiation mismatch). Never merged.
**Discovery.** Multi-scale Laplacian-of-Gaussian search on a smoothed DEM; depressions and mounds need depth/diameter >= 0.09 and a closed rim; valleys and pit chains from elongated curvature structures. Candidate boundaries, slope, roughness, relief from the DEM; everything not in the inputs stays missing. Mission filters apply before scoring. Regional scans only; not a global search.
**Physics.** Bekker-Wong rigid-wheel terramechanics solved by vectorised bisection; quasi-static slip on slope; RK4 transients. Assumptions: flat soil, no grousers, gravity-independent soil parameters, constant slip. Soil sets are literature-cited or illustrative.
**Accuracy reporting.** Verification (closed-form and invariant checks) vs validation (against reality). Physics: 11 verification checks. Similarity: 6 consistency checks (no ground-truth labels exist). Detector: precision/recall on synthetic planted features across degradation levels. None of this is validation on real hardware or real terrain.
**Limits.** Earth cannot reproduce target gravity, vacuum, radiation or atmosphere; spectral curves are schematic unless real library spectra are registered.
